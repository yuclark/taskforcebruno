P033 [normal] (Drawing=False): Preface
P034 [normal] (Drawing=False): The coexistence of students, faculty, and free-ranging campus animals has become a defining and beloved aspect of the Cebu Institute of Technology - University (CIT-U) culture. However, as the population of these "University Pets" grows, so does the complex responsibility of ensuring both animal welfare and public health. While initiatives like Task Force Bruno and the Catch, Neuter, Vaccinate, and Return (CNVR) framework have laid the groundwork for compassionate population control, the administrative execution has been hindered by fragmented, paper-based record-keeping and siloed communication.
P035 [normal] (Drawing=False): The Wildcat Welfare Portal was conceptualized to bridge this gap. This capstone project introduces a centralized, web-based digital governance structure designed exclusively for the CIT-U premises. By transitioning manual logs into a unified digital ecosystem, the portal equips Task Force Bruno, the Medical-Dental Clinic (MDC), and student advocates with the tools they need to make proactive, data-driven decisions.
P036 [normal] (Drawing=False): This document serves as the foundational blueprint for the Wildcat Welfare Portal. Its primary purpose is to align all stakeholders—including our Development Team, university administration, clinic personnel, and academic evaluators—under a shared, easily understood vision.
P037 [normal] (Drawing=False): In the following pages, we detail the system's core architecture and its five primary functional pillars:
P038 [normal] (Drawing=False): Instant Identification: QR-coded profiles for real-time access to a pet’s health history.
P039 [normal] (Drawing=False): Clinical Governance: A secure MDC Dashboard for logging medical incidents and vaccination schedules.
P040 [normal] (Drawing=False): Campus Vigilance: A community newsfeed for crowdsourced reporting of sightings and welfare updates.
P041 [normal] (Drawing=False): Humane Rehoming: A digital adoption pipeline that tracks the end-to-end journey of rehoming pets.
P042 [normal] (Drawing=False): Resource Transparency: A live inventory tracker to ensure the sustainable distribution of essential supplies.
P043 [normal] (Drawing=False): Importantly, this system is designed as a supplemental digital layer; it does not replace the critical physical operations and vetting processes managed by Task Force Bruno. Instead, it aims to eliminate administrative friction, replacing guesswork with measurable metrics.
P044 [normal] (Drawing=False): Through this documentation, we establish the quantifiable performance factors necessary for a successful deployment, ensuring that CIT-U not only manages its campus animals effectively but sets a modern standard for what it means to be a truly pet-friendly institution.

P065 [normal] (Drawing=False): The Wildcat Welfare Portal is a web-based platform created specifically to manage free-ranging dogs and cats
P066 [normal] (Drawing=False): located within the grounds of CIT-U University. This system will be used by Task Force Bruno (i.e., MDC, PCO,
P067 [normal] (Drawing=False): and SSD staff), potential adopters, student volunteers, and members of the surrounding university community.
P068 [normal] (Drawing=False): The system consists of 5 primary functionalities:
P069 [normal] (Drawing=False): QR Coded Pet Profiles: Digitized identification system that allows for instant access to a campus pet’s vaccination record, health history, and behavior notes by scanning the QR code.
P070 [normal] (Drawing=False): MDC Medical Dashboard: Restricted to clinic staff who log, manage, and track the medical records of pet-related visits, vaccination reminders, and report cases of bites or scratches.
P071 [normal] (Drawing=False): Community Newsfeed and Reporting Hub: A “live” module for reporting animal sightings outside the campus, posting missing pet alerts, and posting updates on animal welfare.
P072 [normal] (Drawing=False): Digital Adoption Pipeline: An integrated adoption application system that allows end-to-end tracking of how to rehome pets from their profile page.
P073 [normal] (Drawing=False): Resource and Donation Tracker: “live” inventory tracking dashboard that has an optional online payment option that tracks and monitors the distribution of food and medical supplies.
P074 [normal] (Drawing=False): Visitors may not bring their personal pets onto campus, nor will animals located outside CIT premises be
P075 [normal] (Drawing=False): included in this system. The Task Force Bruno will continue to be responsible for the physical vetting of all
P076 [normal] (Drawing=False): applicants to adopt animals from the MDC; this will not be a part of the MDC's clinical operations, but will serve
P077 [normal] (Drawing=False): as a supplemental digital layer for documentation purposes only.
